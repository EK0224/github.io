INDEX DESIGN
- No cover images
- No excerpts
- Year on the left
- Title + date + category on the right
- Newest first

ARTICLE FOLDERS
Use:
YYYY-MM-DD-Vn

Example:
essays/2026-08-01-V1/

A text-only essay needs only:
index.html

If an essay needs images:
essays/2026-08-01-V1/
├── index.html
├── 01.jpg
├── 02.jpg
└── 03.jpg

The Essays index does not need to know whether an article has images.

The sample dates/titles in data/essays.json are placeholders for layout testing.
Replace them with the real dates before publishing.
